# Workshop Recording: Introduction to Using Power BI to Create Data Visualizations (Nov. 19, 2025) 

This page provides a video recording of an introductory workshop on using Power BI for data visualization.

**Workshop Description:**

* **What:** Learn to use a market\-leading, point\-and\-click data visualization tool, [Power BI](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/power-platform/products/power-bi), by creating visualizations, such as bar graphs, line graphs, pie charts, and scatter plots.
* **Why:** Learning data visualization skills and tools will help you gain insights into your data, communicate your findings, and stand out in the job market.
* **Software:** [Power BI Desktop](https://mdlutoronto.github.io/power-bi-desktop-installing/) is free to everyone at U of T.
* **Who:** This workshop is designed for those new to Power BI Desktop and data visualization. Skills gained can help participants from any discipline: Humanities, Social Sciences, or Sciences. There are no prerequisites or assumptions of knowledge of statistics or programming.
* **How:** This is a 1\.5\-hour workshop, consisting of a short lecture and then a series of demonstrations, which you can follow along with or just watch, interspersed with self\-paced activities. There are no breaks.
